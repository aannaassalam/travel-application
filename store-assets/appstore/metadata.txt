DESCRIPTION (4000)
Flexi Agency puts your whole trip in one app. Search flights, book hotels,
order from restaurants, reserve buses, cars and activities across the
Democratic Republic of the Congo — then keep every booking, price and
document in one place.

PLAN AND BOOK IN ONE PLACE
• Flights, hotels, buses, car hire, property and activities in a single app
• Real listings with photos, amenities, ratings and firm prices in US dollars
• Filter and sort by price or rating — no switching between sites and agents
• Book in a few taps and see your confirmation straight away

EVERY BOOKING, ALWAYS AT HAND
• "My bookings" keeps upcoming and past trips in order
• Status at a glance: confirmed, unpaid, documents issued
• Open any order for full pricing, travellers and travel documents
• Pay online, or settle by mobile money or cash

FOOD, DELIVERED OR ON THE TABLE
• Browse restaurants in Kinshasa, Goma, Lubumbashi and beyond
• Full menus with prices, delivery zones and delivery times
• Build your order, add it to the cart and pay in the app

SAVE WHAT YOU LIKE
• Tap the heart to save hotels, restaurants and activities
• Build a shortlist before you commit
• Your saved list is there on your next trip

TALK TO A REAL AGENT
• Reach the Flexi Agency team on WhatsApp or by phone, from inside the app
• Ask about a booking, a change, or something you cannot find online
• Human help when you want it, self-service when you don't

BUILT FOR THE DRC
• French and English, switchable any time
• Prices in US dollars
• Hotels listed with the details that matter locally: generator, borehole,
  secure parking, air conditioning, airport shuttle

WHY FLEXI AGENCY
Most travel apps solve one piece. You end up with a flight in one app, a hotel
confirmation in your email, a restaurant order on WhatsApp and a voucher
somewhere in your downloads. Flexi Agency brings it together: one account, one
list of bookings, one clear view of what is paid and what is still pending —
with a real agency behind it when a screen is not enough.

Whether it is a weekend in Goma, a work trip to Lubumbashi or a family journey
across the country, Flexi Agency takes you from idea to booked, and keeps
everything tidy afterwards.
